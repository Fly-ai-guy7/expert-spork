## 2. Positioning

RxEgypt is a locally-built (AISE / Misr) digital-health platform improving
medicine access, pharmacy efficiency, and patient safety in Upper Egypt / Red
Sea region. It is **regulation-first**: Rx gating, PDPL consent, and bilingual
health-information disclaimers are built in, not bolted on.

### UNDP angle — combined pitch
Pair **RxEgypt** (pharmacy + patient access) with **Seha.io** (WhatsApp health
triage) to present an end-to-end, low-bandwidth digital-health pathway suitable
for underserved communities — a stronger UNDP Digital Health narrative than
either product alone.
